How It Began

A Grandmother's Recipes, A Son's Dream

In 2010, Meenakshi Achi moved from Karaikudi to Chennai and missed the food of home. With her son Karthik, she opened a 12-seat mess on a side street in Anna Nagar. There was no menu board — just whatever Achi cooked that morning.


Word spread through office-goers and college students. By 2015 we had moved to a proper restaurant, and in 2019 we opened the rooftop that made us famous. Today, Achi's rules still run our kitchen.

"If the masala isn't fresh, the food isn't honest."
— Meenakshi Achi, Founder
