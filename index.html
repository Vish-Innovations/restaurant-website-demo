Our Story

From a 12-Seat Mess to Chennai's Favourite Chettinad Table

Kaaram & Co. began in 2010 as a tiny mess in Anna Nagar run by Meenakshi Achi and her son Karthik. The rule was simple — no ready-made masala, ever. Fifteen years later we still roast and grind every spice blend in-house each morning.
